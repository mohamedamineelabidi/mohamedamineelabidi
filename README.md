# Mohamed Amine El Abidi

Data and AI engineer, Rabat. I build the systems behind LLM products: agents, retrieval pipelines, and the data layer they run on.

At Upfund I work on AI agents with Vertex AI and Firebase and on the FastAPI backend on GCP. Before that, at Valhko, I built ARYA, a recruitment platform run by six cooperating agents.

## Projects

- [CasaMotion](https://github.com/mohamedamineelabidi/CasaMotion): real time mobility platform for Casablanca. Matches riders to vehicles in under 5 seconds and forecasts demand with ML. Kafka, Flink, Spark, Cassandra, Grafana.
- [Multiagent-Recruitment](https://github.com/mohamedamineelabidi/Multiagent-Recruitment): ARYA. Six LangGraph agents cover CV parsing, role matching, screening, job demand forecasting and KPI reporting. FastAPI, Azure, Docker, CI/CD.
- [Big-Data-Procurement-Pipeline](https://github.com/mohamedamineelabidi/Big-Data-Procurement-Pipeline): batch pipeline for retail procurement across 15 points of sale and 5 warehouses. Hadoop, Trino, Airflow, Docker.
- [ethereum-whale-tracker-predictor](https://github.com/mohamedamineelabidi/ethereum-whale-tracker-predictor): benchmarks RNN, GRU, LSTM and XGBoost on 48h of on-chain and market features to predict large ETH moves. Streamlit app with a per model leaderboard.
- [smart-network-assistant](https://github.com/mohamedamineelabidi/smart-network-assistant): telecom support assistant that reads router LEDs from the phone camera with computer vision, then walks the customer through the fix.
- [career-pipeline](https://github.com/mohamedamineelabidi/career-pipeline): scores job offers against a CV and tracks applications. Drafts, never sends.
